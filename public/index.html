FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know before you join.

How does the 7-day free trial work?

Every new account gets 7 days of full Premium access automatically — Study, N&MC Practice, AI Tutor, Daily Tutorial, Nutrition, Abroad, Community and premium News. No card is required to start the trial. After 7 days, Premium tools lock until you subscribe.

How much is Premium and how do I pay?

Premium is GHS 50/month. Payment is handled through Paystack — cards and mobile money are supported depending on your Paystack checkout options. We never see or store your card details; Paystack processes the payment directly.

Do I need Document Evaluation before I apply abroad?

It's not required, but strongly recommended. The Hub team reviews your transcripts, certificates and profile fit for GHS 450 before you spend time and money applying — so you know exactly where you stand first.

Are the practice questions official N&MC or NCLEX past papers?

No. All practice questions, theory prompts and the exam simulation are original, Hub-authored content written in a licensing-style format. They are study preparation tools, not reproductions of any official exam paper.

Is the AI Tutor a substitute for clinical advice?

No. The AI Tutor and Daily Tutorial are educational study tools only. They never provide individualized diagnosis, prescribing or clinical decision-making — always follow your institution's official teaching and current professional guidance in real practice.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Manage or cancel your subscription from your Profile page at any time — there's no lock-in contract.

How do I reach support?

Sign in and use the in-app Enquiries desk for the fastest response, or email nursesmidwiveshub@gmail.com directly.
